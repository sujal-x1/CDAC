In this example , in the second round when we add one field
	private double salary

even after making this change , deserialization does not fail ,SerialVersionUID will not be different this time as we have declared "SerialVersionUID" in the class.

But that does not mean whatever changes we do in the class after serialization will be allowed during deserialization. There is a separate topic "compatible and incompatible changes" we'll see later where we will get to know which changes are allowed and which are not despite declaring "SerialVersionUID" in the class.
