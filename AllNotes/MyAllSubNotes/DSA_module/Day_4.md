## Tree
- Non linear heirarchical structure, mostly dynamic but can be made static
- a tree can have atleast utmost 1 root
- grows with branches and ends with leaves
- Applications: autocomplete, autocorrect , large scale data sorting and searching , heirarchical storage of the  cloud , Ai algos like branch and bound called decision tree.
- Trees are commonly used in cloud storage , searching functionality and heirarchical storage on infra model.
- Height and depth of a tree is length of longest running branch(Height is taken from bottom to top (leaf to root) and depth top to bottom)
